02 — About

Hi, I’m Rae.

I grew up spending summers flipping over rocks on the Maine coast, and I’ve been drawing what I found under them ever since. Tidepool is my one-person print studio in a converted mill building in Worcester.

Every print is made on a risograph, a 1980s Japanese printer that works like a tiny screen-print shop. Each color is its own pass of soy-based ink, so no two prints line up exactly the same. That wobble is the best part.
