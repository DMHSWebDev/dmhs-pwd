Free for our community

Free Web Design Services

Our students design and build real websites for local businesses, nonprofits, clubs, and people in the Worcester community — at no cost. You get a website. They get real-world experience for their portfolios.

01

Showcase

FREE

A clean, professional website that tells people who you are and what you do.

  • A few pages, like Home, About, and Contact
  • Looks great on phones, tablets, and computers
  • A gallery to show off your photos or work
  • Links to your email, phone, and social media
  • Loads fast so visitors don’t leave

Great for: portfolios, résumés, artists, clubs, events

02 Recommended

Storyteller

FREE

Everything in Showcase, plus a site you can update yourself. No tech skills needed.

  • Everything in Showcase
  • A blog or news page to share updates
  • A simple dashboard to add posts, photos, and changes yourself
  • A contact form that sends messages straight to you
  • A sign-up list so customers can hear from you
  • A guestbook where visitors can leave kind words
  • Menus, services, or products you can change anytime

Great for: small businesses, restaurants, coaches, nonprofits

03

Storefront

FREE

A complete online business. Sell products, take payments, and let customers sign in.

  • Everything in Storyteller
  • An online store with a shopping cart
  • Secure checkout that accepts credit and debit cards
  • Customer accounts with log-in and order history
  • Add products, prices, and photos from your dashboard
  • Members-only pages for signed-in customers
  • A message board where your community can post and chat

Great for: shops, makers, merch, bakeries, ticket sales
