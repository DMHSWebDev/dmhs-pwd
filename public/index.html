About the program

A state-approved Chapter 74 CTE program

Programming & Web Development is one of Doherty Memorial High School’s Chapter 74 Career & Technical Education programs, approved by the Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education (DESE) and offered through Worcester Public Schools.

Our coursework follows the state’s Programming and Web Development framework, part of the Information Technology Services career cluster. Students earn their high school diploma while building the skills that colleges and employers look for, and they practice them on real projects for real clients.

What students learn

  • HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
  • Server programming with Node.js and Express
  • Responsive, accessible web design
  • Graphic design and user experience (UX)
  • Databases, APIs, and working with data
  • Cybersecurity and safe coding habits

Real-world experience

  • Free websites for Worcester businesses, nonprofits, and clubs
  • Working with clients from first meeting to launch day
  • Team projects with version control (Git and GitHub)
  • A professional portfolio of live, published work

Where it leads

  • College programs in computer science, IT, and design
  • Careers like web developer, software developer, and UX designer
  • IT support, cybersecurity, and digital media roles

Interested in joining? Learn more about Programming & Web Development at Doherty on the Worcester Public Schools website.

What it costs to keep your site running

We build your website for free. Like any website, it then needs a web address and a place to live online, and those come with small costs. Here is what to plan for.

Your first year

Building your site is free, and so is our help for the first year after launch. Our students fix problems and make updates for you. Help follows the school calendar, so summer requests may wait until fall.

Running costs

  • Web address (like yourname.com): about $10–20 a year
  • Hosting: often free for a Showcase site, about $7–25 a month for a site with a dashboard or store
  • Card payments (Storefront): about 2.9% + 30¢ per sale, charged by the payment company

Most accounts are set up in your name, so you stay in control of your site. We’ll go over how hosting is set up and paid for when we plan your site.

After the first year

Want us to keep helping? Our yearly support plan covers fixes and updates all year for a yearly fee. Ask about it when you request your site. Prefer to run it yourself? We’ll show you how before your first year ends.
