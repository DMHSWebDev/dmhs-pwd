Our story

Started in a home kitchen, still made by hand.

Ember & Oat began as a Saturday table at the farmers market, with six loaves and a borrowed tent. Now we bake about four hundred loaves a week, plus pastry, from our little shop on Elm Street. The recipes haven’t changed much. The oven got a lot bigger.

“If it isn’t worth the wait, we don’t bake it.”
